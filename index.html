What to Expect

A Simple, No-Pressure Format

Every month follows a relaxed structure that's easy to walk into — even if it's your first time.

01

Arrive & Mingle

Doors open at 6 PM. Grab a drink, meet people organically — no name-tag speed rounds, just real conversation.

02

Featured Speaker

A local professional shares their story, lessons, and takeaways in a casual, engaging talk — followed by open Q&A.

03

Keep the Conversation Going

After the talk the floor is yours. Exchange contacts, swap ideas, or just enjoy the company. No awkward exits.
